Install in three steps

Android

1Download

Tap Download for Android. If your browser warns about the file, choose Download anyway. Orione Play is not on the Play Store yet.

2Allow the install

Open the file. When Android asks, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source, then go back.

3Open and enjoy

Tap Install, then Open. Future versions install over it and keep your library.
